## Props

Other props, such as `className` or `data-*` attributes, are forwarded to the root element.

`id` is also the base of the ids generated for tabs and panels.

`Tabs.Tab` must be a direct child: any other child is ignored.

<Controls />

## Keyboard

- `Tab`: moves focus to the selected tab, then to its panel.
- `ArrowRight`: moves focus to the next tab and selects it. From the last tab, moves to the first.
- `ArrowLeft`: moves focus to the previous tab and selects it. From the first tab, moves to the last.
- `Home`: moves focus to the first tab and selects it.
- `End`: moves focus to the last tab and selects it.
